About Nova Space

Creating spaces,
creating comfort.

We're a home décor and office interiors studio based in Jabi, Abuja — run by a small team who believe a well-placed vase or the right shelf can change how a room feels.

Every piece we stock is chosen for the same three things: it should be stylish, it should be functional, and it should still look right in five years. That's the whole brief.
